Gardens & Heat

New York City’s Heat Vulnerability Index (HVI) shows which neighborhoods are most at risk of heat-related illness and death. It combines environmental and social conditions into a five-level ranking, helping identify where heat exposure and residents’ ability to stay safe overlap. Understanding this geography is important for directing cooling resources and examining how community gardens fit within the city’s broader heat-resilience network.
