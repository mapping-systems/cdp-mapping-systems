# Inclusion and Categorization Criteria

The map documents the concentration and variety of design-related resources in New York City. It is an exploratory inventory, not a claim that every relevant resource has been captured.

## Learn

Physical NYC institutions or programs offering recurring education in design, architecture, visual communication, interaction, fashion, industrial design, or related fields.

## Experience

Public museums, galleries, archives, and cultural institutions with substantial design, architecture, material-culture, or visual-culture programming.

## Make

Physical makerspaces, fabrication laboratories, workshops, and shared facilities with prototyping or production tools. Access conditions should be recorded.

## Connect

NYC-based organizations and communities offering recurring design events, professional programming, exhibitions, or networking.

## Verification

- Confirm an official website or authoritative listing.
- Confirm that the organization is active.
- Confirm a physical location or clear NYC presence.
- Preserve the original source and source identifier.
- Remove duplicates across sources.
- Record uncertainty rather than guessing.
