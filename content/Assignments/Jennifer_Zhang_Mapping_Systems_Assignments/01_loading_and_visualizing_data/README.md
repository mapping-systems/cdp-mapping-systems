# 01 · Loading and visualizing data

Research question: **How does the published inventory of public Wi-Fi differ across New York City's five boroughs, both in the concentration of recorded hotspots and in their listed access type?**

The executed notebook focuses on two attributes, `boroname` and `type`. It contains an official-boundary choropleth, a citywide point map, a count-versus-density comparison, and a 100% stacked access-type chart. Inline notes explain why each representation was chosen, how normalization changes the comparison, and what the published inventory can and cannot establish.

## Files

- `01_nyc_wifi_access.ipynb` — executed submission notebook.
- `build_notebook.py` — reproducibly constructs the notebook before execution.
- `data/nyc_wifi_hotspots.csv` — reproducible NYC Open Data snapshot downloaded 2026-08-07.
- `data/nyc_borough_boundaries.geojson` — official Department of City Planning borough boundaries downloaded 2026-08-07.
- `outputs/` — four figures, borough summary CSV/GeoJSON, and cleaned hotspot GeoJSON generated by the notebook.

Sources: [NYC Wi-Fi Hotspot Locations (dataset ID `yjub-udmw`)](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/d/yjub-udmw) and [Borough Boundaries (dataset ID `gthc-hcne`)](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/d/gthc-hcne).
