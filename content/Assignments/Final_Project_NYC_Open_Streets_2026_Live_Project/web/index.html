METHOD

What “ten-minute access” means here

The map uses an 800-meter buffer around the complete Open Street line, based on an explicit assumption of 80 meters per minute for ten minutes.

This is a straight-line approximation, not a pedestrian-network route. It may cross barriers such as highways, rail infrastructure, water, or inaccessible blocks.

Read the map carefully

The analysis measures listed locations and approved schedules. It does not establish actual opening, staffing, visitor counts, safety, comfort, program quality, or accessibility for different mobility needs. NYC DOT notes that hours may change and include setup or breakdown time.
